Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Siarhei Trukhin ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use Word Moment (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Usage Data. We automatically collect information about how you use the App, including the features you use, the time and frequency of your sessions, and crash reports. This data is used to maintain and improve the App.

App Installation Identifiers. The Firebase services used by the App may generate a per-installation identifier (a Firebase Installation ID, and where Analytics is enabled an app-instance identifier). These identifiers identify an installation of the App on a device rather than you by name.

Device and Technical Data. We may receive device model, operating system version, app version, language, timezone and approximate country. Where Firebase Performance Monitoring is in use, your IP address may be processed to derive approximate country and to help prevent abuse.

Push Notification Registration. If you enable notifications, a per-installation messaging token is generated so that messages can be delivered to your device. This token is used for technical transmission of notifications.

The App does not request names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, or other directly identifying personal information from users.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Send technical notifications and updates where notifications are enabled
  • Monitor and analyse usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Deliver push notifications to your device where you have enabled them
  • Measure App performance and diagnose slow screens or failing network requests
  • Detect, investigate and fix crashes and technical problems

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may process information about users:

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, provided by Google as part of the Firebase platform — We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the App, and Firebase Crashlytics to detect and diagnose crashes. Analytics may use an app-instance identifier and process information such as sessions, device model, operating system and approximate geography. Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, application state, device metadata and a Crashlytics installation identifier. The App does not use these services for advertising or behavioral advertising. The Android Advertising ID (AAID) is not collected or transmitted by the App. Firebase's privacy documentation is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Firebase Cloud Messaging, provided by Google as part of the Firebase platform — We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications. It may process a per-installation Firebase Installation ID and a messaging token to determine which devices should receive notifications. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Firebase Performance Monitoring and Remote Config, provided by Google as part of the Firebase platform — We use these services to measure App performance and to change App behaviour without shipping an update. They may process a per-installation Firebase Installation ID, device and operating system details, app and package version, network request URLs, response codes and timings, and an IP address for purposes including deriving approximate country. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or allow access to information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers, including Google Firebase services, that help us operate and improve the App
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Siarhei Trukhin, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law

The App does not use collected information for advertising or behavioral advertising.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the App's services, maintain and improve the App, and comply with our legal obligations.

Where data is processed by Google's Firebase services, Google applies its own documented retention periods. Examples published by Google include: Crashlytics keeps crash stack traces for 90 days before beginning removal; Performance Monitoring keeps IP-associated events for 30 days and installation-associated data for 60 days; and data held for Cloud Messaging and Remote Config is removed within 180 days of a deletion request. Current periods are published at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at razrabnerab@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days, or within any shorter period required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Word Moment is a general audience application intended for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13, and is made available without regional restrictions, including within the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland.

The App does not request or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, or any other directly identifying personal information from any user, including children.

The App does not use, collect, or transmit the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or any other advertising identifier, and does not collect SIM Serial, Build Serial, BSSID, MAC address, SSID, IMEI, or IMSI. The App contains no advertising and builds no advertising or behavioural profile of any user, including children.

Push notifications ("Word of the Day") are delivered using Firebase Cloud Messaging topic subscriptions. Devices subscribe to a topic through the Firebase SDK; we do not operate a server-side database of individual device tokens or installation identifiers, and we cannot identify, locate, or single out a specific individual from our side. Technical identifiers referenced in Section 1 (such as the Firebase Installation ID or messaging token) are generated and processed by Google's Firebase infrastructure for the purposes described in Section 3; we do not receive, store, or have access to these identifiers ourselves.

Because we do not hold any identifier linking a specific individual or device to data processed by Firebase, we are typically unable to locate or action an individual deletion request ourselves. Any data processed by Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config) is retained and removed according to Google's own applicable retention policies described in Section 5. Uninstalling the App stops the App from generating and transmitting new data from that installation. Disabling notifications stops the App from subscribing to or delivering further push notifications to that device.

If you are a parent or guardian with questions about how the App processes technical data, or if you believe the digital consent age applicable in your country requires additional consent for this processing, you may contact us at razrabnerab@gmail.com. We do not currently offer an age-verification or parental-consent mechanism, as the App does not collect any data that would allow us to identify a user as a child in the first place.

For significant changes, we will provide additional notice where appropriate. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
