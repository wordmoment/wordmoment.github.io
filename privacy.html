Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Moment (the "App"), developed by Siarhei Trukhin ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Usage Data. We automatically collect information about how you use the App, including the features you use, the time and frequency of your sessions, and crash reports. This data is used to maintain and improve the App.

App Installation Identifiers. The Firebase services used by the App may generate a per-installation identifier (a Firebase Installation ID, and where Analytics is enabled an app-instance identifier). These identifiers identify an installation of the App on a device rather than you by name.

Device and Technical Data. We may receive device model, operating system version, app version, language, timezone and an approximate country derived from your IP address. This is standard behaviour of the Firebase services described below, and is used for diagnostics and usage statistics rather than to locate any individual user.

Push Notification Registration. If you enable notifications, a per-installation messaging token is generated so that messages can be delivered to your device.

The App does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, or precise (GPS-based) location. Some of the technical data described above — such as installation identifiers or an IP-derived approximate country — may still be considered personal data under certain laws, even though it does not by itself reveal your name or contact details. The App does not collect or transmit an advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / AAID).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Send technical notifications and updates where notifications are enabled
  • Monitor and analyse usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Measure App performance and diagnose slow screens or failing network requests
  • Detect, investigate and fix crashes and technical problems

We do not use any of the above for advertising, ad targeting, or building an advertising profile — the App does not display ads and has no advertising integrations.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google's Firebase platform for several standard technical functions:

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics — used to understand general usage patterns and to detect and diagnose crashes. May process an app-instance identifier, session data, device model, operating system, and an approximate country derived from IP address.

Firebase Cloud Messaging — used to deliver push notifications, via a per-installation token that determines which devices receive a given message.

Firebase Performance Monitoring and Remote Config — used to measure app performance and to adjust app behaviour without shipping a new release. May process an installation identifier, device/OS details, network request timings, and an IP-derived approximate country.

We do not use Firebase-generated identifiers (such as the Firebase Installation ID, the Crashlytics Installation UUID, or a messaging token) to identify a user by name or contact information, and none of these services are used by us for advertising. These identifiers are generated and used internally by Google's Firebase services to operate features such as crash reporting, analytics, and notification delivery; we do not maintain a separate database on our side mapping these identifiers to individual users. The App does not collect or transmit the Android Advertising ID (AAID). Firebase's own privacy documentation, including current data handling and retention practices, is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share or allow access to information in limited circumstances:

  • With Google/Firebase, as a service provider that helps us operate the App (see Section 3)
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Siarhei Trukhin, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law

The App does not use collected information for advertising or behavioural advertising, and does not sell data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and improve the App, and to meet our legal obligations. Data processed through Google's Firebase services is subject to Google's own retention schedules, which Google may update from time to time; current details are published at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Broadly, this data is retained for a limited period (typically on the order of weeks to a few months depending on the service) rather than indefinitely.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a portable copy of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at razrabnerab@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, and in any event within any period required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Word Moment is a general-audience application suitable for a wide age range, including children, and is made available without regional restriction.

The App does not request or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, or other directly identifying information from any user, including children. The App does not collect or transmit an advertising identifier, contains no advertising, and does not build an advertising or behavioural profile of any user.

Push notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging topic subscriptions. We do not maintain our own database on our side linking individual devices or people to a topic subscription. Technical identifiers such as the Firebase Installation ID or a messaging token are generated and processed within Google's Firebase infrastructure for the purposes described in Section 3; we do not separately store or have direct access to these identifiers ourselves.

Because these identifiers are not held or linked to a specific person on our side, we are typically not able to action individual deletion requests for that data ourselves — it is retained and removed according to Google's own schedules referenced in Section 5. Uninstalling the App stops new data from being generated by that installation; disabling notifications stops further push delivery to that device.

The App does not currently use age verification or collect information to establish a user's age. It is designed to avoid collecting information that directly identifies users, regardless of age. If you are a parent or guardian with questions about how the App handles technical data, contact us at razrabnerab@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Websites

The App links to a support website, available at both wordmoment.razrabnerab.workers.dev and a mirror at wordmoment.github.io. The App itself does not collect any data when you open these links — it simply opens the page in your browser, choosing the mirror based on your device's language setting.

Once you're on either page, the underlying hosting provider (Cloudflare Workers or GitHub Pages) may process standard web request data, such as your IP address, for its own operational and security purposes, independently of the App. This is standard behaviour of any website and is governed by the hosting provider's own policies, not by Word Moment. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement for details.
